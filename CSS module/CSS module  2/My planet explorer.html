My planet explorer

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known astronomical object in the universe capable of harboring life. It is a terrestrial, rocky world categorized as the fifth largest planet in our solar system by both mass and size.

Mars

fourth planet from the Sun. Known as the "Red Planet" due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface, it is a cold, rocky desert about half the size of Earth with two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun, and the largest in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a mass nearly 2.5 times that of all the other planets in the Solar System combined and slightly less than one-thousandth the mass of the Sun.
